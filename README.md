# Mathematical-Logic-EFT-
This repo contains my study notes of the textbook Mathematical Logic by H.-D. Ebbinghaus, J. Flum and W. Thomas, Springer, 2nd edition, as well as my attempt at solving the exercises and filling in the gaps (i.e., mathematical statements without proofs).

# To Compile
Put main.tex and chapter1.tex,...,chapter13.tex in the same folder and use LaTeX.
Note:
1. Ignore appendixa.tex, which is an incomplete attempt at proving the formal arithmetics within Peano's axioms for Gödel's Second Incompleteness Theorem (X.7.10). I recommend consulting the book, Lectures in Logic and Set Theory. Volume I: Mathematical Logic by George Tourlakis, for a complete development of such formal arithmetics; chapter 2 in the book gives a complete proof of the second incompleteness theorem.
2. The file "FO and Equality.tex" contains a proof that the notion of equality cannot be axiomatized in the fragment of first-order logic without equality; in other words, the stipulated interpretation of the equality symbol as equality, the specific binary relation over a certain domain (cf. Definition III.3.2), is essential.
3. A discussion of the (\\/S) rule from the sequent calculus for first-order logic introduced in Chaper IV of the textbook (cf. Section 6 in that chapter for a list of all sequent calculus rules) is given in the file chapter4.tex, which proves that the two forms of (\\/S) are indispensible, i.e., one cannot drop either form and still have a complete sequent calculus for first-order logic (completeness is shown in Chapter V of the textbook).
